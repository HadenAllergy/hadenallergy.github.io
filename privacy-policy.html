Haden Allergy App

Privacy Policy — Effective March 2026

1. Overview

Haden Allergy ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data Collected

The App may store the following data locally on your device: your approximate geographic location (latitude/longitude, used for pollen data retrieval); medication names, dosages, and reminder schedules you enter; your allergen profile selections; and your display preferences. No data is collected, transmitted to, or stored on any server controlled by the App developer(s).

3. Third-Party API Requests

To provide pollen data and geocoding, the App sends API requests to:

Google Pollen API — receives geographic coordinates only; used for high-resolution pollen forecasts. See Google Privacy Policy.

Open-Meteo — receives geographic coordinates only; used as a fallback pollen data source. See their terms.

Nominatim/OpenStreetMap — receives city/location search queries. See their privacy policy.

No personal identifiers, health data, or medication information is ever sent to these services.

4. Local Storage

All user data is stored locally on your device. This data is not encrypted beyond your device’s built-in protections and may be accessible to anyone with physical access to your device. The App does not store protected health information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. The App is not a HIPAA covered entity.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This App does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or any form of cross-site tracking. No user behavior data is collected or transmitted.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Data persists until you clear it. You may delete all App data at any time by using the "Clear All Data" button on the Info tab, or by deleting the app from your device.

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from children. Pediatric allergy management should be supervised by a qualified healthcare provider.

8. Data Breach

Because no data is stored on external servers, there is no server-side breach risk. Users are encouraged to use device-level security (screen lock, encryption) to protect stored data.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: access all data the App has stored; delete all stored data at any time; and use the App without providing any personal data (functionality will be limited).

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The effective date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
